The 14-in-1 In-Browser PDF Document Studio

Amazing-Tools PDF Studio provides an enterprise-grade suite of PDF manipulation tools that execute 100% inside your browser. Unlike conventional online PDF converters that require you to upload private contracts, tax returns, and identity documents to third-party cloud servers, our architecture leverages WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript (pdf-lib and pdf.js) to process your documents locally.

Key PDF Capabilities Available:

  • Edit & Sign: Add text annotations, insert drawn signatures, type names, or place transparent signature stamps directly onto contracts.
  • Merge & Split: Combine dozens of PDF files into a single master document, or extract custom page ranges (e.g. pages 1, 3, 5–10).
  • Compress & Optimize: Reduce bloated PDF file sizes for email attachments and government portal uploads without severe visual degradation.
  • Organize & Rotate: Reorder pages, rotate inverted landscape scans 90° or 180°, and delete unwanted pages with real-time thumbnail previews.
  • Protect & Unlock: Encrypt PDF files with passwords or strip permissions restrictions from your own files for effortless sharing.
  • Convert: Convert PDF pages to high-resolution JPG images, or compile multiple JPG/PNG images into a clean PDF booklet.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Are my uploaded PDF files safe from data leaks?

Yes. In fact, your PDF files are never "uploaded" anywhere. The computation happens entirely within your computer's browser memory. When you close the tab, nothing remains on any server.

Is there any file size limit for PDF processing?

Because processing uses your local device's memory, you can comfortably process PDF files up to 100MB+ depending on your system RAM. There are no arbitrary server upload restrictions.

Is this PDF suite free for commercial and business use?

Yes! The PDF Studio is completely free to use directly in your browser. If you need bespoke custom document automation workflows or internal tools for your company, check out our custom development packages.
