Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 20, 2026

At Amazing-Tools ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data. All 42 tools on Amazing-Tools process data entirely within your client web browser. No text, images, spreadsheets, PDFs, or calculations are ever uploaded to any server.

We may collect anonymous, aggregated information through third-party analytics services, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • Referral source (how you found us)

2. Cookies

We may use cookies and local browser storage for:

  • Essential preferences: Storing your selected color theme, user interface states, and preferences locally in localStorage.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand aggregated site usage and optimize page performance (e.g., Google Analytics).

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not impair the core client-side processing of any tool.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: Collects anonymous usage data. See Google Analytics Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare / GitHub Pages: Secure, encrypted SSL content delivery infrastructure.

4. Data Security & 100% Client-Side Architecture

Since all tool processing happens entirely in your browser memory, your data never leaves your device. We do not operate databases that store your files or calculation inputs. We implement modern Content Security Policies and HTTPS encryption to protect website delivery.

5. Children's Privacy

Our website is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data through our tools, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. For any analytics data concerns, you can:

  • Opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on
  • Clear browser storage and cookies anytime through your browser settings

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
