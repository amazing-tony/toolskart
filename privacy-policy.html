Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

At ToolsKart ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data. All tools on ToolsKart process data entirely within your web browser. No text, images, files, or calculations are uploaded to any server.

We may collect anonymous, aggregated information through third-party analytics services, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • Referral source (how you found us)

2. Cookies

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies for:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function properly.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the site (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Advertising cookies: Used by our advertising partners (e.g., Google AdSense) to show relevant advertisements.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense: Displays advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics: Collects anonymous usage data. See Google Analytics Privacy.

4. Data Security

Since all tool processing happens in your browser, your data never leaves your device. We do not have access to any text, images, or files you process using our tools. We implement reasonable security measures to protect our website infrastructure.

5. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data through our tools, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. For any analytics data concerns, you can:

  • Opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on
  • Manage ad preferences at Google Ad Settings
  • Clear cookies through your browser settings

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
