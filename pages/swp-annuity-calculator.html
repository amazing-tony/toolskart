What is a Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP)?

An SWP allows you to withdraw a fixed amount of money from your mutual fund investments at regular intervals (monthly, quarterly, or annually). While you withdraw money, the remaining balance continues to generate market returns, significantly delaying or preventing capital exhaustion.

Why Inflation-Indexed Annuities Matter

A fixed pension of ₹40,000/month might feel comfortable today. However, with 6% annual inflation, that same ₹40,000 will have the purchasing power of only ₹12,400 after 20 years. Planning with an annual payout increase ensures your dignified lifestyle remains unbroken well into your 80s.
