Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a resume ATS-friendly?

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) scan for clean, standard typography, distinct section headings (Experience, Education, Skills), simple layouts without complex graphical tables, and text that can be parsed directly. This tool generates clean, standard ATS-friendly PDF outputs.

Are my resume details stored on any server?

No. All data is processed entirely in your web browser. Nothing is uploaded to any remote server or third-party database.
