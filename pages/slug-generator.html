Why are URL Slugs Important for SEO?

A URL slug is the exact address of a specific page on your website (e.g., in example.com/blog/url-slug-generator, the "url-slug-generator" is the slug).

Search engines like Google read your URL slugs to understand what the page is about. A good slug should be:

  • Readable: Easy for humans to read and understand.
  • Descriptive: Contains your primary keyword.
  • Short: Avoid stop words (a, an, the) to keep it concise.
  • Hyphenated: Google recommends using hyphens (-) instead of underscores (_) to separate words.

Frequently Asked Questions

Hyphens vs. Underscores: Which is better?

Google explicitly recommends using hyphens (-) for URLs. Search engine crawlers treat a hyphen as a space, meaning url-slug is read as "url slug". An underscore is not treated as a space, so url_slug would be read as one continuous word "urlslug".

What does transliteration mean?

Transliteration converts characters from one script or language into characters of another. In our tool, it changes accented letters (like á, é, ñ, ü) into their standard Latin equivalents (a, e, n, u) so they can be safely used in URLs.

How does this tool process text?

It first converts everything to lowercase, replaces special characters via transliteration (if selected), removes any non-alphanumeric characters, and replaces spaces with your chosen separator while ensuring no duplicate separators exist.
