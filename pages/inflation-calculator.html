Inflation & Purchasing Power Formulas

Future Cost: F = P × (1 + r)n

Past Purchasing Power: P = F / (1 + r)n

Where:
P = Present amount
r = Annual inflation rate (as a decimal, e.g. 6% = 0.06)
n = Number of years

Frequently Asked Questions

What is purchasing power risk?

Purchasing power risk is the danger that cash, fixed deposits, or conservative returns will not keep pace with rising inflation, eroding the real basket of goods and services your money can buy over time.

How can I beat inflation?

Equities, index mutual funds, diversified real estate, and inflation-indexed bonds historically offer returns that outpace inflation over long 10+ year horizons.
