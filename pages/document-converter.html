Frequently Asked Questions

Are my documents private and secure?

Yes, 100%. Unlike conventional web converters, Amazing-Tools processes files entirely in your browser using client-side libraries. No data is ever transmitted or stored on any server.

Can I convert scanned PDFs into Word or Excel?

For scanned image-only PDFs, text extraction requires Optical Character Recognition (OCR). If your PDF contains selectable text, it is extracted cleanly into editable paragraphs and tables.

Is there a file size limit?

Because conversions occur in your computer's memory, you can comfortably convert files up to 50 MB depending on your device RAM.
