Frequently Asked Questions

What does "percent" mean?

Percent comes from the Latin "per centum", meaning "by a hundred". It is a number or ratio expressed as a fraction of 100.

How is percentage change useful?

Percentage change is widely used in finance and statistics to show how much a value has grown or shrunk over time relative to its original amount.

Can I use negative numbers?

Yes, for most calculation modes (like percentage change), negative numbers can be used to reflect losses or negative balances.
