💡 3 Golden Rules of Prepayment

1. Prepay Early in the Loan: In the first 5 years, over 70% of your EMI goes into interest alone. Prepaying ₹1 Lakh in Year 2 saves twice as much interest as prepaying in Year 15!
2. The 1 Extra EMI Hack: Paying just 1 extra EMI every year on a 20-year home loan reduces tenure by nearly 4 years and saves up to ₹11+ Lakhs in interest.
3. Zero RBI Prepayment Penalty: Per RBI regulations, banks cannot charge any prepayment penalty on floating-rate individual home loans. You can prepay ₹1,000 or ₹10 Lakhs anytime for free.

How Does Loan Prepayment Save So Much Money?

When banks calculate your EMI using a monthly reducing balance method, interest is charged every single month on the outstanding principal balance. In the initial years, because the balance is high, the interest constitutes the largest chunk of your monthly installment.

When you make any prepayment (whether an extra ₹5,000 every month or an annual bonus), 100% of that prepayment goes directly towards reducing the principal balance. This shrinks your remaining loan amount immediately, which permanently decreases all future interest charges for the remaining tenure.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Should I reduce loan tenure or reduce monthly EMI?

Reducing tenure is mathematically far superior because you eliminate months or years of compound interest, saving the maximum money. Reducing EMI keeps the original loan length but lowers your monthly cash flow requirement — best if your current household expenses are tight.

Is it better to prepay my home loan or invest in SIP?

If your home loan interest rate is above 8.5%–9.0%, prepaying provides a guaranteed, risk-free, tax-free return equal to your loan rate. If you have high risk tolerance, investing that surplus into an Equity Mutual Fund SIP delivering 12%–14% CAGR historically creates more net wealth, but comes with market volatility and requires discipline not to stop during market dips.

Can I make multiple prepayments in a single year?

Yes. You can make prepayments as often as you want through your bank's netbanking or branch. You can do a monthly top-up plus an annual lumpsum bonus together.
