🧠 Goal-Based Investing: The Secret to Long-Term Wealth

Most people invest blindly without knowing why they are investing or when they need the money. Goal-based financial planning ties every rupee you save to a specific milestone in your life.

  • Never mix short-term and long-term goals: Goals under 3 years (e.g. Home Down Payment) should be in liquid/arbitrage funds or fixed deposits, not equity.
  • Account for realistic inflation: General CPI inflation is ~6%, but education inflation is 9-10% and healthcare inflation is 12-14%. If you don't inflate costs, your target corpus will fall drastically short.
  • Increase your SIP by 10% every year (Step-up): As your salary grows, stepping up your SIP ensures you hit all your life targets 5 to 7 years ahead of schedule.
