How to Use the Regex Tester

  • Step 1: Type your regular expression pattern in the top input field. Do not include the starting and ending forward slashes (they are provided).
  • Step 2: Check or uncheck the flag boxes (Global, Case Insensitive, Multiline, Single Line) to modify how the search behaves.
  • Step 3: Type or paste your test text in the large text area.
  • Step 4: Matches will instantly highlight in blue/green, and detailed information about capture groups and indices will appear in the results list below.

Common Regex Flags Explained

g (Global): Don't return after the first match. Find all matches in the document.

i (Insensitive): Case-insensitive matching. It makes [A-Z] match both uppercase and lowercase letters.

m (Multiline): Changes the behavior of ^ and $ to match the start and end of a line, instead of just the start and end of the entire string.

s (Single Line / DotAll): Allows the dot (.) to match newline characters.

Frequently Asked Questions

What engine does this Regex Tester use?

This tool relies on your browser's native JavaScript RegExp engine. It supports standard JavaScript regex syntax, including lookaheads, lookbehinds (in modern browsers), and non-capturing groups.

What are Capture Groups?

Capture groups are created by placing parts of your regex inside parentheses (). They group multiple tokens together and "capture" the matched text so you can reference it later or extract specific parts of a string. Our results section will display each captured group under its main match.
