Why Adding Brand Watermarks and Logos Matters

Visual content theft is rampant across social platforms, e-commerce stores, and stock image repositories. Photographers, small business owners, and real estate agencies routinely find their original imagery republished without permission or attribution. Stamping a transparent brand logo or copyright badge across your imagery deters unauthorized scraping and reinforces brand recognition.

Zero-Upload Browser Security

Cloud-based watermarking platforms often retain rights or log metadata from your high-resolution portfolios. Amazing-Tools renders your watermarks directly onto your photos using HTML5 Canvas alpha compositing in your local browser memory. No data is ever transmitted across the network.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I use transparent PNG logos?
Yes! Transparent PNG and SVG logos are fully supported and will seamlessly overlay with pristine alpha transparency over your base photos.
Does watermarking reduce the base photo's original resolution?
No. The canvas renders precisely to your original photo's full pixel width and height, ensuring zero resolution loss.
