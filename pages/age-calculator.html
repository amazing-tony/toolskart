How to Use the Age Calculator

  • Step 1: Select your Date of Birth.
  • Step 2: Select the date you want to calculate your age as of (defaults to today).
  • Step 3: Click "Calculate Age" to see your exact age in various units and countdown to your next birthday.

How It Works

The Age Calculator computes the exact difference between the two dates provided. It calculates full years, remaining months, and remaining days. It also converts the total time span into total months, total weeks, total days, and total hours for interesting statistics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Age Calculator free to use?

Yes, our Age Calculator is completely free to use without any limits.

Can I calculate age for past or future dates?

Absolutely. You can change the "Calculate Age As Of" date to any past or future date to determine age at that specific time.

How is the next birthday calculated?

It calculates the difference in days from the "Calculate As Of" date to your upcoming birthday in the same or next year.
