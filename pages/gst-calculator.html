  • Step 5: Click "Calculate GST" to see the detailed tax breakdown.

GST Calculation Formula

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is calculated differently based on whether you're adding or removing tax:

Adding GST:
GST Amount = Original Price × (GST Rate / 100)
Total Price = Original Price + GST Amount

Removing GST:
Original Price = GST-Inclusive Price / (1 + GST Rate / 100)
GST Amount = GST-Inclusive Price − Original Price

For intra-state transactions, GST is split equally between CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST). For inter-state transactions, the full rate applies as IGST (Integrated GST).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GST?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a comprehensive indirect tax on the supply of goods and services in India. It replaced multiple cascading taxes and was implemented on July 1, 2017. GST is levied at every stage of the supply chain with credit available for taxes paid at earlier stages.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST) are levied on intra-state transactions — when the buyer and seller are in the same state. IGST (Integrated GST) is levied on inter-state transactions — when goods/services move between different states. The total tax rate remains the same; only the split changes.

What are the current GST slabs in India?

India has four main GST slabs: 5% (essential items like packaged food, transport services), 12% (processed food, computers, umbrellas), 18% (most services including IT, telecom, financial services, restaurants), and 28% (luxury items like automobiles, aerated drinks, premium goods). Some items like fresh food, milk, and grains are GST-exempt (0%).

How do I remove GST from a price?

To find the original price before GST, divide the GST-inclusive price by (1 + GST rate/100). For example, if an item costs ₹1,180 including 18% GST: Original Price = ₹1,180 / 1.18 = ₹1,000. The GST amount is ₹180.
