Tax Rules at a Glance

  • Gratuity: 100% tax-free for Government employees; exempt up to ₹20 Lakhs for non-government employees under Section 10(10).
  • Leave Encashment: Fully exempt for Government employees; exempt up to ₹25 Lakhs for non-government employees under Section 10(10AA).
  • EPF: 100% tax-exempt principal + interest after 5 continuous years of service.

How Gratuity is Calculated in India

For employees covered under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, gratuity is computed using the formula:

Gratuity = (15 × Last Drawn Basic + DA × Years of Service) / 26

Here, 26 represents working days in a month, and 15 represents 15 days of salary per year of completed service. Any service exceeding 6 months is rounded up to the next full year.
