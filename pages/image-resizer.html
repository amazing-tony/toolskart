How to Use the Image Resizer

  • Step 1: Click the upload box or drag-and-drop your image into it.
  • Step 2: Enter your desired width or height. If "Maintain aspect ratio" is checked, the other dimension will calculate automatically.
  • Step 3: Alternatively, click one of the social media presets for instant sizing.
  • Step 4: Click "Download Resized Image" to save it to your device.

Is this tool safe and private?

Yes, 100% safe. This Image Resizer is built using the HTML5 Canvas API, meaning all image processing happens locally in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers, keeping your data entirely private and ensuring lightning-fast results.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Maintain aspect ratio" mean?

The aspect ratio is the proportional relationship between the width and height of an image. Maintaining it ensures your image doesn't look stretched or squished when you change one of the dimensions.

Will resizing reduce the image quality?

Making an image smaller (downscaling) generally preserves quality very well while reducing the file size. However, making an image significantly larger (upscaling) than its original dimensions will cause it to look blurry or pixelated.

What formats are supported?

Our tool supports all standard web image formats that your browser can read, including JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF (though animated GIFs will be downloaded as static images).
