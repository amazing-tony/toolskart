How to Use the FD Calculator

  • Step 1: Enter the Total Deposit Amount (Principal).
  • Step 2: Enter the annual Interest Rate offered by the bank.
  • Step 3: Enter the tenure of the Fixed Deposit in years and months.
  • Step 4: Select the Compounding Frequency (Indian banks mostly use Quarterly).
  • Step 5: Click "Calculate FD" to see your maturity amount and total interest earned.

FD Calculation Formula

Fixed Deposit maturity is calculated using the compound interest formula:

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)

Where:
A = Maturity Amount
P = Principal deposit amount
r = Annual interest rate (in decimal)
n = Number of times interest is compounded per year
t = Tenure in years

Frequently Asked Questions

What is compounding frequency?

It dictates how often the interest is added to the principal balance. Quarterly means interest is calculated and added every 3 months. More frequent compounding leads to slightly higher returns.

What is Effective Annual Rate?

Because of compounding, the actual yield you get in a year is slightly higher than the quoted annual interest rate. This higher rate is the Effective Annual Rate.
