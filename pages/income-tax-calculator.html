Frequently Asked Questions

What is Standard Deduction?

It is a flat deduction from your gross salary. For the New Regime in FY 2025-26, it is ₹75,000, and for the Old Regime, it is ₹50,000.

What is Health and Education Cess?

An additional 4% cess is levied on the calculated income tax to fund health and education initiatives.

Which regime is better?

The New Regime generally offers lower tax rates but fewer deductions. If you have significant investments like 80C, HRA, home loan interest, the Old Regime might be better. You should calculate both to see which lowers your tax liability.
