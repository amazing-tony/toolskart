💡 Which Regime Should You Pick?

When Old Regime is Better: If your total deductions (80C + NPS + 80D + HRA/Home loan interest) exceed ₹3.75–₹4 Lakhs, Old Regime typically saves thousands of rupees.
When New Regime is Better: If you don't have a home loan or high rent, New Regime offers lower tax rates, higher standard deduction (₹75k), and zero tax up to ₹7.75 Lakhs gross income.
