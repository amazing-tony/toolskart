How to Compress Images Online

  • Step 1: Select or drag and drop a JPG, PNG, or WebP image into the upload box.
  • Step 2: Use the quality slider to adjust the compression level. The preview will update automatically.
  • Step 3: Compare the original and compressed sizes to see how much space you've saved.
  • Step 4: Click "Download Compressed Image" to save the optimized file.

Why Compress Images?

Large image files can significantly slow down your website, leading to higher bounce rates and lower search engine rankings. By compressing images, you reduce the file size without noticeable loss in visual quality.

Optimized images also save bandwidth and storage space on your devices and servers, making them much faster to share via email or messaging apps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image uploaded to your server?

No. All compression happens locally inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your images never leave your device, ensuring 100% privacy and security.

Which formats can be compressed?

Our tool currently supports the compression of JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg), PNG (.png), and WebP (.webp) files. Note that PNG compression relies on browser implementations and may sometimes result in larger files if not photographed correctly.
