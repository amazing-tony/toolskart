How RD Interest is Calculated in India

In India, banks and the Post Office calculate interest on recurring deposits compounded on a quarterly basis. The formula used is:

M = P × ((1 + r/4)^(4t) - 1) / (1 - (1 + r/4)^(-1/3))

Where:
M = Maturity Amount
P = Monthly installment
r = Annual interest rate (in decimal)
t = Tenure in years
