How to Use the Word Counter

  • Step 1: Type or paste your text into the text box above.
  • Step 2: The word count, character count, and other metrics will update automatically in real-time.
  • Step 3: Check your keyword density in the top 10 keywords list below the text area.
  • Step 4: Use the "Copy" or "Clear" buttons to easily manage your text.

How Reading and Speaking Times are Calculated

Our tool estimates reading time based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute (WPM), which is standard for adult readers.

Speaking time is calculated based on a conversational speaking rate of 130 words per minute. These metrics are helpful for writing speeches, presentations, or timing blog post reads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool save my text?

No, our Word Counter runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No text is ever uploaded to our servers, ensuring your data remains 100% private and secure.

What is the difference between characters and characters without spaces?

"Characters" counts every single keystroke, including spaces, tabs, and line breaks. "Characters (no spaces)" ignores all white space. Many systems (like SMS or Twitter) count spaces as characters, while some specific academic assignments only count letters.

How does it count sentences?

The tool counts sentences by looking for punctuation marks that typically end a sentence, such as periods, exclamation points, and question marks.
