How to Use the SIP Calculator

  • Step 1: Enter the amount you plan to invest every month.
  • Step 2: Input the expected annual rate of return (historically equity mutual funds yield 10-14%).
  • Step 3: Enter the number of years you want to invest.
  • Step 4: Click "Calculate Returns" to see your estimated total wealth.

SIP Formula

The mathematical formula used to calculate SIP returns is based on compound interest:

FV = P × [((1+r)^n - 1) / r] × (1+r)

Where:
FV = Future Value of the investments
P = Monthly investment amount
r = Monthly rate of return (Annual rate / 12 / 100)
n = Number of monthly installments (Years × 12)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)?

A SIP allows you to invest a fixed amount regularly (e.g., monthly) in a mutual fund scheme. It helps in rupee cost averaging and building wealth over the long term through the power of compounding.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No, SIP returns are generally linked to market performance (like equity or debt markets) and are not guaranteed. The return rate you enter is an estimate based on historical performance or your expectations.

What is the power of compounding in SIP?

Compounding happens when the returns on your investments start generating their own returns. Over a long period, this creates a snowball effect, accelerating the growth of your total wealth.
