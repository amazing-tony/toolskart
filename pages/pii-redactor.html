Why Redacting PII is Essential Before Sharing Text with AI Models

With the explosive adoption of Large Language Models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, Microsoft Copilot, and Google Gemini, developers and corporate employees frequently copy-paste application logs, legal contracts, customer support emails, and database records into web prompts to troubleshoot bugs or summarize meetings. However, transmitting unredacted Personally Identifiable Information (PII) violates strict global data privacy regulations including the European Union GDPR, California CCPA, HIPAA, and corporate confidentiality protocols.

Key Data Elements Automatically Detected:

  • Email Addresses: RFC-compliant email detection replacing personal communication points with structured [EMAIL] tokens.
  • Phone Numbers: International E.164 formats, US numbers with area codes, Indian mobile 10-digit formats, and European phone notations.
  • Financial Payment Cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, RuPay, and Discover credit card number sequences.
  • National Identification: US Social Security Numbers (SSN) and Indian 12-digit Aadhaar formats.
  • Server & Network Infrastructure: Public IPv4 and IPv6 addresses preventing inadvertent internal corporate topology disclosure.
  • Developer Secrets: JWT Bearer tokens, GitHub personal access tokens, AWS keys, and OpenAI sk-... format keys.

Yes! By default, the tool replaces entities with named semantic tokens (like [EMAIL_1], [PHONE_1]). This allows AI assistants like ChatGPT to understand the context and relationships in your text without learning the actual confidential identity of the individual.
