How to Download Videos or Audio from YouTube, Shorts & Instagram

  1. Copy Link: Open YouTube, Instagram, or Facebook and copy the link to the video or Reel.
  2. Paste Link: Paste the URL into the search box above and tap "Download Now".
  3. Choose Quality: Select either Video (1080p, 720p, 480p) or Audio (320kbps MP3).
  4. Save to Device: Click the red Download button next to your desired quality to save the file instantly.
