Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any recording limits or watermarks?

No! You can record high-definition video for as long as your system memory allows. There are no watermarks, no limits, and no accounts required.

Is my screen recording private?

Yes, 100%. The video stream is recorded entirely in your browser using the MediaRecorder API. The footage never leaves your computer and is never sent to any server.

Can I record sound from browser tabs or applications?

Yes. When your browser displays the screen-sharing prompt, check the "Share tab audio" or "Share system audio" box to record sound from YouTube, Zoom, games, or presentations.
