Understanding Mid-Market Exchange Rates

The mid-market rate (also called the interbank rate) represents the midpoint between global buy and sell prices on foreign exchange markets. Traditional retail banks, airport currency counters, and credit card providers often apply hidden markups ranging between 2% and 5% above the mid-market rate. Using our converter helps you verify the true benchmark value of your funds before authorizing international bank transfers or payments.
