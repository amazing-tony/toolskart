Why Brand Your Videos In-Browser?

Video content published on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and LinkedIn is frequently copied and reposted without attribution. Placing an identifiable channel watermark, creator handle, or corporate logo protects your copyright and drives traffic back to your brand.

Conventional video editing programs (like Premiere or DaVinci) are complicated to learn, and cloud video SaaS platforms charge costly monthly subscriptions while uploading your multi-gigabyte video files to their servers. Amazing-Tools processes your video entirely within the local HTML5 Canvas and MediaRecorder APIs for instantaneous client-side results and 100% privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the export process work?
When you click "Record & Auto-Save", the browser plays the video from the beginning and captures the composited canvas frames into a high-quality WebM video using hardware-accelerated MediaRecorder encoding.
Are videos ever uploaded to a server?
Never. All video decoding, logo compositing, and stream encoding happen strictly inside your browser memory.
